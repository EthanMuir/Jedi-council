The Guide

How the Council actually works: what the colors mean, how to use each screen, and what every Council Member does and is allowed to see.

Reading a Verdict

BULLISH
expects the price to rise
BEARISH
expects the price to fall
NO_READ / NO_CONVICTION
abstained -- data didn't support a call, or an audit gate blocked one

This mapping is the same everywhere -- seat chairs in the Chamber ring, the center holocron, the dissent map, the holo-panel, and every seat's vote in the Crypt. A seat abstaining is a deliberate, valid answer in this system -- it is not a failure state, it means the seat's own data genuinely didn't support a directional call for this ticker and horizon.

The Screens

The Chamber

  1. Type a ticker (fixture data only covers NVDA until a real market-data provider key is configured in .env -- everything else will run the full pipeline but fail at the data-fetch step).
  2. Pick a horizon: Next Day / Next Week / Next Month / Next Year. Not every seat is competent at every horizon -- see the competence table below -- so fewer seats light up at 1d than at 1y.
  3. Click Convene the Council. Seats light up live as each finishes its isolated read (Phase A, the blind round). Click any seat chair to open its full reasoning in the holo-panel.
  4. Once all Tier I seats report: the Reality Anchor appears (Phase B), then the Bull/Bear debate with the Prosecutor's rulings (Phase C), then the audit gates and Risk Warden's position size, and finally the Grand Master's synthesis fills the center holocron (Phase F). The whole run is written to the Crypt automatically (Phase G).

The Crypt

  1. Every deliberation ever run lives here, permanently -- the ledger is append-only and hash-chained, so nothing can be edited or quietly deleted after the fact.
  2. Filter by ticker and/or horizon, then click Update the Crypt to sweep any prediction whose resolution window has passed and score it against what actually happened.
  3. Click a card to see every seat's individual vote alongside the Grand Master's final verdict for that prediction.
  4. The scoreboard compares the Council's blind vote, final (weighted) vote, and extremized vote against two baselines: buy-and-hold and always-bullish.

The Archives

  1. Once real resolutions accumulate in the Crypt, this shows each seat's actual track record: hit rate, Brier score, log loss, and a calibration curve (predicted probability vs. how often that was actually right).
  2. Rank (Youngling → Grand Master) requires at least 20 resolved predictions before it can move at all -- a lucky streak cannot mint a Grand Master, and a seat's Crypt-derived weight in future votes only starts shifting once it clears that same floor.

The Oracle

  1. Runs the exact same pipeline as the Chamber, plus a free-form question field.
  2. A ticker is still required -- every Tier I seat's data domain (OHLCV, filings, options, ...) is fetched per-ticker, so there's no ticker-free market data to isolate seats around.
  3. Your question is handed to the Grand Master as explicit framing for the final synthesis only -- it does not change what any Tier I seat itself is allowed to see or how it votes.

Which Seats Vote at Which Horizon

A hardcoded competence weight per seat per horizon (0.0-1.0). A seat at 0.0 is not called at all -- no tokens spent on an opinion that horizon's own spec says the seat has no business having. Higher weight also means more influence in the final weighted vote, before calibration and data-quality adjustments are applied on top.

Tier I -- The Twelve Seats

Every seat below deliberates in total isolation -- it is constructed with only the data fields listed, enforced in code (not by prompting), and is sampled independently three times to measure its own run-to-run consistency before its votes are combined with anyone else's.

Keeper of the Charts
technician
Sees: price/volume (OHLCV) only. The ticker itself is anonymised to "Instrument A" so the model can't pattern-match the real name from training data.
Does: direction is decided by a deterministic family-vote engine (trend, breakout, oscillator, volume), never by the LLM -- its job is only to narrate the setup and place entry/exit/invalidation from ATR and structure.
Keeper of the Ledgers
fundamentalist
Sees: financial statements, margins, growth, and valuation multiples vs. sector. No price action at all.
Does: a fundamentals read. Weak at short horizons by design -- abstains entirely at 1d.
Watcher of Omens
catalyst_seer
Sees: the news/sentiment feed only.
Does: classifies each catalyst SCHEDULED vs SURPRISE and must justify why a headline isn't already priced in.
Student of the Inner Circle
insider_reader
Sees: Form 4 insider transactions only.
Does: weighs cluster buys over single buys, C-suite over directors, open-market purchases far over option exercises; treats routine 10b5-1 sales as near-zero signal.
Reader of the Republic
senate_watcher
Sees: congressional disclosure filings and pending legislation/regulation relevant to the ticker.
Does: must explicitly account for disclosure lag -- filings appear well after the underlying trade happened.
Reader of Great Tides
flow_cartographer
Sees: 13F institutional holdings, ETF inclusion, short interest, days-to-cover.
Does: must note the quarterly lag on 13F data and discount its own confidence accordingly.
Reader of Probabilities
oracle_options
Sees: the option chain / IV surface / put-call ratio only.
Does: its most valuable output is the implied expected move, not direction -- every other seat's price target gets checked against this seat's distribution downstream.
Keeper of the Outer Rim
macro_sage
Sees: yields, curve shape, Fed path proxies, CPI, employment, the dollar, oil, and the ticker's historical beta to the market -- never the ticker's own price series.
Does: a macro backdrop read. Strong at 1m/1y, usually abstains at 1d.
Reader of Distant Stars
cross_market
Sees: the sector ETF, a peer basket, an index-futures proxy, and an overseas-session proxy -- never the ticker's own price series.
Does: looks for confirmation (or contradiction) from a genuinely different market, on the theory that same-asset data is already priced in.
Keeper of Expectations
estimate_scribe
Sees: analyst estimates, revision history, price-target dispersion, guidance vs. consensus, historical surprise pattern.
Does: weighs revision momentum and analyst dispersion more heavily than the raw estimate level.
Listener to the Council of Officers
transcript_linguist
Sees: earnings call transcripts (prepared remarks and Q&A) only.
Does: looks for hedging-language shifts, guidance verb changes, analyst tone, and avoided topics -- always compares the last four calls, never one in isolation.
Keeper of Charters
structure_archivist
Sees: SEC filings (8-K, S-1, S-3, 13D/G), buybacks, dilution, debt maturities, covenants, ESG/litigation flags.
Does: low frequency, high impact -- abstains often, and that's correct behaviour for this seat, not a failure.

Tier II -- The Adversarial Chamber

Bull Advocate & Bear Advocate
bull_advocate / bear_advocate
Sees: all twelve Tier I verdicts (vote, probability, thesis, data quality) -- never the raw underlying data.
Does: each builds the strongest possible case for its assigned side regardless of personal read. The assignment is fixed, not chosen -- they are advocates, not analysts.
The Devil's Advocate
prosecutor
Sees: everything -- every Tier I verdict, the debate so far, and which direction the council is currently converging on.
Does: attacks the converging direction, whichever it is. Hunts for evidence cited by multiple seats (counted once, not once per seat), price targets outside the plausible range, and theses that don't actually depend on the data shown. Has veto power -- can force the final verdict to NO_CONVICTION.

Tier III -- Quality Control

None of these four are an LLM call -- pure computation, deterministic, no token cost.

Keeper of What Usually Happens
base_rate_keeper
Computes: the ticker's unconditional historical return distribution -- hit rate of "up," realised volatility, ATR-implied range, max plausible move.
Used for: the Reality Anchor -- every seat's price target is checked against it, and targets far outside it are flagged IMPLAUSIBLE.
Keeper of the Toll
cost_auditor
Computes: spread as a percentage of expected move, round-trip cost, break-even move.
Does: if the edge is smaller than the cost of trading it, the verdict is downgraded to NO_CONVICTION regardless of how enthusiastic the rest of the council is.
Keeper of the Shields
risk_warden
Computes: ATR/IV-based position size for a fixed risk budget, with a fractional-Kelly cap and a concentration check against other open Crypt positions.
Does: sizing only, never direction -- its own function signature takes no vote or probability as input, so it is structurally incapable of sizing based on conviction.
Keeper of the Crypt's Memory
calibration_officer
Computes: from the resolved Crypt, per-seat hit rate, Brier score, log loss, and a calibration curve (what The Archives page displays).
Does: outputs a weight vector for the next deliberation's vote -- every seat starts at 1.0, and weight can only move once a seat has at least 20 resolved predictions, so a lucky streak can't buy influence early.

Tier IV -- Synthesis

Master of the Order
grand_master
Sees: everything -- every Tier I verdict, the full Bull/Bear debate, every Prosecutor ruling, the Reality Anchor, and the Cost Auditor's result.
Does: writes the final call. Must report the structure of disagreement, not just a consensus number -- unanimity among seats that all leaned on the same correlated evidence is treated as a warning sign, not a green light. Cannot override a Prosecutor veto.
