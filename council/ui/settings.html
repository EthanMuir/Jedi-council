Free Mode

Off

Runs every seat on free AI models: Google's Gemini, with Groq taking over if Gemini's free daily limit runs out mid-run. Costs nothing.

What you give up
  • Weaker answers. Free models miss more nuance than Claude and follow the Council's rules less reliably, so more seats may sit a run out.
  • Less independent. Every seat runs on the same kind of model, so their disagreements mean less.
  • Limits. Free Gemini allows about 15 requests a minute, so runs are slower and seats sometimes pause with a countdown. There's also a daily cap per model, set by Google (the Rate limits page in Google AI Studio shows yours). Lite runs use about a third of the requests, and a free Groq key adds more.
  • Privacy. On its free tier, Google may use what you send to improve its products.
  • Scored separately. Free runs are labelled in History and kept apart in the Seat record.
