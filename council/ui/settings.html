Free Mode

Runs every seat on free AI models -- Google's Gemini, with Groq taking over if Gemini's free daily limit runs out mid-run. Costs nothing.

The honest trade-off
  • Weaker answers. Free models miss more nuance than Claude and follow the Council's rules less reliably, so more seats may sit a run out.
  • Less independent. Every seat runs on the same kind of model, so their disagreements mean less than on a mixed council.
  • Limits. Free Gemini allows only about 15 requests a minute, so runs are slower and seats sometimes pause to wait (they show a countdown). There's also a daily cap per model, which Google sets and changes -- the Rate limits page in Google AI Studio shows yours. Lite runs use about a third of the requests, and a free Groq key adds more.
  • Privacy. On its free tier, Google may use what you send to improve its products.
  • Scored separately. Free runs are labeled in the Crypt and kept apart in the Archives, so they never mix with paid results.
